# Eligibility Verification Dashboard Specification

## Purpose

This dashboard shows how eligibility status affects claim outcomes, rework, days in A/R, and patient billing clarity.

## Core Metrics

| Metric | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| Verified accounts | Shows accounts with confirmed coverage |
| Inactive eligibility accounts | Shows avoidable front-end risk |
| Eligibility-related denials | Shows downstream claim impact |
| Average days in A/R by eligibility status | Shows reimbursement friction |
| Patient impact notes | Shows patient-facing consequences |

## Recommended Visuals

1. KPI Cards
   - Verified accounts
   - Inactive eligibility accounts
   - Eligibility-related denials
   - Average days in A/R

2. Bar Chart
   - Eligibility risk by payer type

3. Table
   - Inactive eligibility accounts with service line, payer, claim status, days in A/R, and patient impact

4. Workflow Notes Panel
   - Where eligibility verification should be rechecked
   - Which accounts need pre-service correction

## Portfolio Insight

Eligibility verification is not a one-time administrative task. It is a timing-sensitive control point that protects clean claims and patient trust.
