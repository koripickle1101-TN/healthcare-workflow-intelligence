# Brand Rules

## Portfolio Identity

Healthcare Workflow Intelligence is a student-built healthcare administration portfolio focused on revenue cycle workflow analysis, patient access breakdowns, denial prevention, prior authorization visibility, and healthcare operations improvement.

## Primary Brand Colors

- White: #FFFFFF
- Tennessee Orange: #FF8200
- Black: #111111
- Deep Gray: #333333
- Warm Gray: #E8E2DC

## Visual Direction

Use a premium editorial healthcare strategy style.

Recommended design elements:

- High-contrast editorial serif headlines
- Clean sans-serif supporting text
- White background with warm gray structure
- Tennessee Orange accent system
- Double-ring circular nodes with soft glow
- Dotted or fading connector lines
- Structured grid with intentional asymmetry
- 40 to 50 percent whitespace
- Minimal LinkedIn and GitHub icons

## Tone Standard

The writing should be clear, professional, practical, and grounded in healthcare operations. The voice should sound like a healthcare administration student building real operational insight from coursework, patient experience, and workflow analysis.

## Content Rule

Every piece of content should answer one of these questions:

- Where does the workflow break?
- Why does rework happen?
- What downstream impact does the breakdown create?
- What control point would reduce the problem?
- What would a healthcare leader need to see to trust the recommendation?
