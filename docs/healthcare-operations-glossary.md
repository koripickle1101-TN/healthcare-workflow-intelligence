# Healthcare Operations Glossary

## Purpose

This glossary supports healthcare administration, revenue cycle, patient access, prior authorization, denial prevention, and workflow intelligence learning.

## Terms

### Accounts Receivable

Money owed to the organization after services are provided and claims or patient balances are pending.

### Clean Claim Rate

The percentage of claims submitted without errors, edits, or missing information.

### Denial Prevention

The process of identifying and correcting upstream workflow issues before claims are denied.

### Eligibility Verification

The process of confirming whether a patient has active coverage and whether the payer information is accurate before service.

### Front-End Revenue Cycle

The early part of the revenue cycle, including scheduling, registration, intake, insurance capture, eligibility verification, and authorization.

### Prior Authorization

Payer approval required before certain services are delivered or reimbursed.

### Rework

Extra manual effort required to correct an issue that could have been prevented earlier in the workflow.

### Revenue Integrity

The practice of ensuring documentation, coding, charging, billing, and compliance align correctly so revenue is accurate and defensible.

### Root-Cause Analysis

A structured process for identifying why a problem happened instead of only correcting the visible outcome.

### Workflow Control Point

A checkpoint that prevents incomplete, inaccurate, or unclear information from moving downstream.

### Workflow Drift

Gradual movement away from the intended process due to workarounds, unclear ownership, system gaps, or operational pressure.

### Workflow Trust Failure

A point where staff no longer trust the official system and begin using manual workarounds, duplicate checks, or side tracking.
