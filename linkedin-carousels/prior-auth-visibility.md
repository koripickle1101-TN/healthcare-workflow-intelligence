# LinkedIn Carousel Script: Prior Authorization Visibility

## Slide 1

Prior Auth Problems Are Usually Visibility Problems

## Slide 2

When authorization status is unclear, teams rely on manual checking.

That creates delay, duplicate work, and patient frustration.

## Slide 3

A strong prior auth workflow shows:

- Owner
- Status
- Payer requirement
- Submission date
- Due date
- Escalation trigger

## Slide 4

Without visibility, the workflow becomes reactive.

Staff chase answers instead of managing risk.

## Slide 5

Control point:

A shared authorization tracker with status, owner, due date, and escalation field.

## Slide 6

Prior authorization is not just approval.

It is timing, ownership, documentation, and visibility.
