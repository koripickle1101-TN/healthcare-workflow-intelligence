Trust failure signals:

- Side spreadsheets
- Duplicate checks
- Verbal escalations
- Repeated emails
- Manual reconciliation

## Slide 4

These are not simply staff behavior problems.

They are workflow design signals.

## Slide 5

The better question:

Which part of the workflow lost trust, and when?
