# Carousel Slide Template

## Slide 1: Cover

Title the workflow problem clearly.

Example:

Where Revenue Cycle Problems Actually Start

## Slide 2: The Common Assumption

Show what people usually blame.

Example:

People blame billing when claims fail.

## Slide 3: The Real Breakdown

Show the upstream workflow point that created the issue.

Example:

The problem often began at intake, eligibility, authorization, or documentation.

## Slide 4: Downstream Impact

Connect the breakdown to rework, denials, delays, A/R drag, and patient confusion.

## Slide 5: Workflow Control Point

Show the fix: validation checklist, tracker, status dashboard, ownership rule, or exception queue.

## Slide 6: Strategic Insight

State the professional takeaway.

Example:

Denial prevention is workflow design, not just claim correction.

## Slide 7: Footer / CTA

Created by Kori Pickle

Invite the reader to think about one workflow they would audit first.

## Design Notes

Use:

- White background
- Tennessee Orange accents
- Black headline text
- Warm gray dividers
- Double-ring circular nodes
- Dotted connector lines
- 40 to 50 percent whitespace
- Minimal icons
