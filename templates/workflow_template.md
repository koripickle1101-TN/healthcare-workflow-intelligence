## Operational Impact

Explain how the breakdown creates rework, delays, duplicate entry, manual reconciliation, avoidable denials, A/R drag, or escalation volume.

## Patient Impact

Explain how the breakdown may affect patient access, patient understanding, trust, scheduling, cost clarity, or billing experience.

## Fix or Control Point

Name the practical correction, checkpoint, report, checklist, escalation rule, or dashboard that would reduce the issue.

- Denial rate by reason
- Days in A/R
- Authorization turnaround time
- Eligibility-related denials
- Rework volume
- Escalation frequency
- Patient billing complaints
